01 — The winter problem & platform

Make Skin Salt relevant when thirst is quiet

Electrolytes are associated with heat, sweat, workouts, hangovers, and obvious thirst. Winter removes those cues. People drink more coffee/tea, reach for cold water less, and assume electrolytes can wait until summer.

At the same time, winter creates visible beauty problems: indoor heating, lower humidity, cold air, routine changes, travel, holiday makeup — skin feels tight, looks dull, or a summer routine stops holding.

Opportunity. Don’t convince everyone winter is secretly a sports season. Connect an easy hydration ritual to recognizable winter beauty moments.
Strongest winter position. Forgotten-water behavior + visible winter beauty moments + an easy, enjoyable daily ritual — under the brand platform Look Like You Still Feel. Winter makes the gap more visible: she feels fine, but her face can look tired, dull, tight, or less like herself.

02 — Surface symptom → root desire

Show what winter changed before what Skin Salt does

Forgotten-Water Season is the strategic territory. It is not always the best cold-acquisition hook. She probably doesn’t wake up thinking “I hate how little water I drink.” She’s noticing dullness, tired face, makeup not sitting, or a routine that suddenly stopped working.

Consumer entry: something changed in how I look→ Reframe: environment + drinking behavior changed→ Skin Salt: enjoyable beauty-hydration ritual

Four deep territories

  • Attractiveness — still beautiful / fresh / glowy in winter
  • Confidence — people see the version I feel like inside
  • Effort — beauty feels easy, not another 12-step
  • Control — season doesn’t decide how good I look

Creative principle

  • Visible pain she recognizes
  • Emotional consequence (why she cares)
  • Winter reframe (environment / habit)
  • Skin Salt ritual + proof
  • Desired identity
Deeper conclusion. Winter changes environment, behavior, and beauty experience — but she doesn’t want winter deciding how good she looks. Functional job: enjoyable hydration when thirst is quiet. Emotional job: keep the attractive, confident version of herself when winter makes that harder.

03 — Priority angles (canon)

What we lead with

1 Lead territory

Forgotten-Water Season

Bottle still full at 4PM. Coffee/tea replaced water. Barely thirsty. Message is not medical dehydration — winter makes the habit easier to forget. Taste becomes the cue when thirst is quiet.

Role: behavioral insight + product mechanism. Stronger as reframe after a visible beauty entry.
2 Acquisition

Heater Face

Heat comes on → face feels tight, dull, uncomfortable. She adds creams while the drinking habit disappeared. Skin Salt is an additive inside-the-routine step — not a moisturizer/humidifier/water replacement.

Hooks: “Heater Face season has officially started.” / “The heat came on. My glow clocked out.”

04 — First winter test

Controlled learning, not a production boom

Start with distinct hypotheses (not random ads):

  1. A — Environment changed: “Same skincare routine. Different season.”
  2. B — Heater Face: “Heater Face season has officially started.”
  3. C — Forgotten-Water: “It’s 4:17PM and this is still full.” (show the bottle)
  4. D — No extra product: “I refused to add another serum to my winter routine.”
  5. E — Identity: “Be the girl who still glows in February.” vs Off-Season Glow
  6. Control: winterized 3PM Beauty Swap
Hold offer, LP, traffic quality, casting, and approximate asset length constant. Do not pick a winner on CTR alone. Need hook → product belief → LP conversion → CAC → contribution → early repeat/refund.

Message structure

  1. Show the winter moment (specific observation)
  2. Reframe as habit / routine / environment change
  3. Introduce Skin Salt as enjoyable beauty-hydration ritual
  4. Prove formula, taste, offer, terms clearly
Outcome language: glow, radiant, fresh, smooth, looking like herself. Avoid diagnosing ordinary winter symptoms as electrolyte deficiency or claiming Skin Salt replaces skincare.
Taste is strategic. Knowing she should drink water ≠ wanting to. Quiet thirst → enjoyable taste → repeated ritual → adherence / retention.

07 — Winter subscriber insert (Oct/Nov renewal box)

Concept copy

Front

Your winter skincare routine may be missing the part you drink.

When the weather gets colder, thirst can feel quieter. Coffee and tea replace more drinking occasions. Your water bottle stays full for longer.

At the same time, indoor heating and lower humidity can make tightness, texture, dullness, and makeup changes more noticeable.

That is the Winter Hydration Gap: more effort on the outside, while the water habit quietly gets weaker.

Back

Keep one simple beauty-hydration ritual all winter.

Each stick: 750mg electrolytes (Na/K/Mg); DermaVal, HA, ceramides, vitamin C; zero sugar; a flavored reason to return to water when thirst isn’t reminding you.

First Glass Rule: Choose the first intentional glass you can repeat every day. Add one stick before the day gets busy.

Supports hydration and beauty routine. Does not replace normal water, moisturizer, sunscreen, humidification, or professional care.

Optional D3 gift card: seasonal loyalty gift only — separate from formula, does not renew, label warnings required. Not why Skin Salt works. Final dose/label/QR needs regulatory approval before print.

08 — Kill / demote list

What’s out of the winter canon

Kill as winter leads

  • Refreshment / thirst / post-workout / “summer glow”
  • Sweat-permission / sports salt framing as the ad hero
  • Diagnosing ordinary winter symptoms as electrolyte deficiency
  • Skin Salt replaces moisturizer / water / skincare
  • Vitamin D as the reason Skin Salt works
  • Dramatic New Year transformation promises
  • Writing for under-35 / glass-skin / TikTok-teen voice as default
  • Slogan poetry (“fill the tank”) with no specific moment
  • Overclaiming DermaVal “98%” as consumer skin results
  • Big production / spend expansion before Phase 1 gates
  • Picking winners on CTR alone

Keep only as support / overlays

  • Half of LMNT sodium — objection handling, not lead angle
  • Travel / cabin / hangover — holiday overlays after evergreen works
  • Gifting — revenue overlay in Dec, not psychology lead
  • Energy & focus — broaden audience carefully; don’t reopen sports drink
  • Non-Dry January hijack — prefer First Glass / one-better-habit
  • “Winter Skin Baddie” — test vs elevated Off-Season Glow; don’t force everywhere
Guardrail: Winter is the context, not the claim. Prefer recognizable personal observations over “Winter dehydrates your skin and Skin Salt fixes it.”
