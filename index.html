Winter Strategy Recap

Stop selling sweat electrolytes. Sell heater-season skin hydration for women 51–65 whose cream sits on top.

5 Sep 2026 US-primary DTC Spine 51–65 Angle lock

01 — Executive summary

Winter is a barrier season, not a sports season

The electrolyte category’s mental model is sweat = permission to take salt. When she’s not sweating, salt feels extra or risky — while heating quietly dries her face and cold mutes thirst.

Skin Salt’s winter job is not “refreshing glow electrolytes.” It’s a drinkable winter barrier / skin-hydration ritual for women who already buy creams (and often collagen), whose jars stop working under forced air.

Core thesis. Winter doesn’t give sweat-permission for salt — heating drains skin and cold mutes thirst. Skin Salt is the drinkable winter skin-support stick (HA + ceramides + Vitamin C + DermaVal) with 500 mg sodium (half of LMNT’s typical 1,000 mg) so water works when cream alone can’t.

Hero hierarchy

  1. Skin matrix (HA + ceramides + DermaVal) — why winter
  2. Electrolytes as delivery — how water sticks
  3. Energy & focus — bonus only (don’t lead)

06 — Mechanism

UMP → UMS

UMP. She’s been treating a water-holding / barrier-from-within problem as a “buy a richer cream” problem. Forced-air heat + menopause-era drop in skin’s ability to hold water → topicals sit on top. High-sodium sports electrolytes don’t fit desk/winter life.

UMS. Drinkable skin-support matrix (HA + ceramides + Vit C + DermaVal) on everyday electrolytes (500 mg sodium) so water and skin nutrients get in while cream seals outside.

08 — Copy rules

Sell. Don’t slogan.

Do

  • Halbert-style: specific moment, felt desire, concrete next picture
  • Aggressive / power words OK when they sell
  • Steal: parchment, sits on top, hurts, forced air, never thirsty, family photo, Teams, 500 mg, 90 days

Don’t

  • Slogan poetry (“fill the tank”)
  • Beach / sweat / ice plunge / salty rebellion / glass skin
  • Overclaim Dermaval “98%” as skin results
  • Equate 40 mg HA to higher-dose studies

Sodium / no-sweat script

You’re not wrong — you don’t need a sports salt dump when you’re not sweating. Skin Salt is 500 mg sodium — about half of LMNT’s typical 1,000 mg. Winter reason-to-buy is the skin ingredients; electrolytes help water land. BP / sodium limits → doctor.
