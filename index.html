Winter Angle Playbook

The strategic shift

Winter isn’t the off-season. It’s the strongest skin season.

In summer, the easy sell is thirst: sweat, heat, “refreshing hydration,” electrolytes after a workout. In winter that lever gets weaker — nobody’s reaching for a cold electrolyte drink because they’re hot.

The reframe. Cold air outside and dry heated air inside strip moisture out of skin faster than summer ever does — and because people don’t feel thirsty in the cold, they under-hydrate exactly when their skin needs it most. So we don’t abandon hydration. We drop the “refreshing summer drink” framing and re-aim the same mechanism at winter’s real enemies: dryness, dullness, tightness, accelerated aging, low energy, and holiday pressure to look good.

Primary angles — lead with these

Five always-worth-testing leads

01 Primary

Winter is quietly wrecking your skin

Big idea: Cold wind + indoor heating pull moisture out of your skin all day. Creams sit on the surface; the damage is happening from the inside, at the cell.

Emotional core: Frustration — “I moisturize constantly and my skin is still tight, flaky, and dull.”
Why it’s strong: Same cellular-hydration mechanism as summer, but winter dryness is more visceral and more visible. The hydration angle actually gets better here.
Hook directions:
  • “Your winter skincare is fighting the wrong battle.”
  • “Why your skin gets tight the second you walk indoors.”
  • “Moisturizer can’t reach the layer winter is drying out.”

02 Primary

The dehydration you can’t feel

Big idea: You don’t get thirsty in the cold, so you drink far less than in summer — and your skin, energy, and focus quietly crash. Winter dehydration is invisible until it shows on your face.

Emotional core: “Wait, that’s why?” — a hidden cause for something they’ve been blaming on winter itself.
Hook directions:
  • “You’re dehydrated right now and you don’t feel it.”
  • “The reason your skin looks worse in January has nothing to do with the cold air.”
  • “Winter tricks your body into skipping water.”

03 Primary

Winter face / accelerated aging

Big idea: Dry conditions degrade collagen and elastin faster — fine lines, crepey texture, and that tight, dull “winter face” show up sooner. This is where DermaVal leads instead of supports.

Emotional core: Fear of looking older, made concrete by a season they can point to.
Why it’s strong: In winter you can promote the anti-aging story to the front of the ad instead of leading with hydration. Higher-intent, higher-LTV buyer.
Hook directions:
  • “Winter ages your skin faster than summer sun.”
  • “The fine lines that show up every January.”
  • “Cold-weather aging is a real thing — here’s the fix.”

Supporting angles — rotate + test

Four more to keep in the mix

06 Support

Winter energy & focus

Dark mornings, 4pm slumps, seasonal low energy. Lean on the electrolyte side — focus and all-day energy — to broaden past skin entirely. Good for reaching people who don’t think of themselves as “skincare buyers.”

Hook: “Why winter afternoons wipe you out — and the fix isn’t caffeine.”
Keep as support for ICP 51–65 — don’t let this reopen a sports-drink frame as the hero.
07 Support

Post-holiday recovery

Travel, flights, alcohol, sugar, and salt over the holidays all dehydrate and dull the skin. Position January as the reset.

Hook: “Undo the holidays in a glass.” Pairs with New Year.
08 Support

Gifting

Stick packs are a natural beauty stocking-stuffer / gift for the wellness friend. Revenue angle more than psychology — gift bundle, “give the gift of glow,” ride through December.

SocialEmailGift-set offer
09 Support

Dry-hands-and-lips proof of concept

A small, concrete winter tell everyone recognizes — cracked lips, dry hands, tight cheeks — as visible proof that surface products aren’t enough. Useful as a hook into any primary angle.

How to sequence the season

When each angle owns the calendar

  • Late Nov – Dec Lead with Holiday Glow and Gifting. Winter Dryness (#1) is the evergreen cold-traffic workhorse underneath.
  • Late Dec – Jan Pivot hard to New Year Glow-Up + Post-Holiday Recovery. Push subscription.
  • All winter Winter Dryness (#1), Invisible Dehydration (#2), and Winter Aging (#3) are your three always-on cold-traffic angles — test them head to head and scale the winner.
Bottom line. You’re not fighting the season, you’re using it. Winter gives skin a villain people can see and feel, and gives you built-in deadlines (holidays, New Year) that summer never does.

Copy rules

Sell. Don’t slogan.

Do

  • Halbert-style: specific moment, felt desire, concrete next picture
  • Aggressive / power words OK when they sell
  • Steal: parchment, sits on top, hurts, forced air, never thirsty, family photo, Teams, 500 mg, 90 days

Don’t

  • Slogan poetry (“fill the tank”)
  • Beach / sweat / ice plunge / salty rebellion
  • Overclaim Dermaval “98%” as skin results
  • “Get your husband back” / sexual attention as primary

Sodium / no-sweat (still matters in winter)

You’re not wrong — you don’t need a sports salt dump when you’re not sweating. Skin Salt is 500 mg sodium — about half of LMNT’s typical 1,000 mg. Winter reason-to-buy is the skin ingredients; electrolytes help water land. BP / sodium limits → doctor.

Standup line

Winter: stop selling sweat electrolytes. Sell heater-season skin hydration for 51–65 — dryness / invisible dehydration / winter aging always-on, holiday + New Year for deadlines, Jan as the steal month.
